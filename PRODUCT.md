# GulCraftStories, the product

What the site is for, who it is for, and the decisions that shape it. The
visual system is in `DESIGN.md`; conventions and technical detail are in
`CLAUDE.md`. Read this first.

## The brand

GulCraftStories is a handmade jewellery and craft brand made by one person,
Guljeet Kaur, in London, since December 2024. Gul means flower. Every piece is
made by hand, one at a time, and never made again, so whatever someone takes
home is the only one of its kind. Beads, thread, crochet and hand-painted
clay, drawing on the colours and festivals of South Asian and Tibetan craft.

## Where people meet it

Mostly in person: Chiswick Flower Market, the Piccadilly and Kensington
markets, and exhibitions. Instagram (@gulcraftstories) is the second door.
The site is the third, for that same audience: affluent, design-literate,
people who read before they buy and who value a made thing and the person
who made it.

## What the site has to do

1. Show the pieces beautifully and honestly, one photograph at a time.
2. Let someone buy a piece in one step, or ask about it, without a basket,
   an account or a discount in sight.
3. Say where she will be next.
4. Tell her story in her own words.
5. Be easy for her and her son to run from a phone: mark a piece sold, add
   a piece, print price cards.

## Register and references

Register: brand, not shop. It should feel tasteful and gallery-like, the way
an exhibition catalogue or a quiet maker's own site does. Whitespace, one
serif, plain sentences, text links.

Anti-references: busy Etsy-style listing grids, generic Shopify templates,
anything that feels like a discount shop. So: no badges, no countdowns, no
"Shop now", no pill buttons, no gradients or drop shadows, no stock icons,
no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no uppercase labels.

## Decisions that are settled

- **One of one.** Every piece is made once. Sold pieces stay on show, faded,
  with "Sold" after the name. Collection pieces (magnets, clips, charms,
  ornaments, rings) exist in small numbers and are bought with a quantity
  chosen on the payment page.
- **Buying.** "Buy this piece, £49" goes straight to Stripe's hosted page.
  No basket, no accounts. Mix-and-match offers are Stripe Payment Links.
- **Prices in pounds only.** No currency switcher.
- **Contact.** Instagram first, then WhatsApp, then email. Text links, no
  floating buttons.
- **Photos.** 4:5 portrait, uncropped, on a plain background; the pipeline
  pads other shapes and never crops.
- **Domain.** gulcraftstories.com is the main domain; gulcraftstories.co.uk
  redirects to it.
